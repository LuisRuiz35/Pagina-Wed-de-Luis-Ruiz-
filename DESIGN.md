## Brand & Style

The brand personality merges clinical rigor with compassionate human warmth. Tailored specifically for comprehensive physical and cognitive rehabilitation in a regional context, the visual language departs from sterile, intimidating institutional hospital layouts. Instead, it creates an environment that communicates dignity, progression, vitality, and safety.

The target audience encompasses patients undergoing recovery, family caretakers, pediatric and geriatric cases, corporate occupational health partners, and medical referrers. The user experience must evoke optimism, absolute clinical trust, clarity of care, and accessibility.

The aesthetic integrates **Modern Warm Healthcare** with **Soft Ambient Tactility**:
- Crisp, clinical structure balanced by gentle, organic curvature.
- Generous breathable whitespace paired with cool, therapeutic ice-blue tinted atmospheres.
- High-contrast visual cues that guide users with reduced mobility or vision impairments seamlessly toward care coordinators and direct instant communication channels.
- Elegant script touches reserved exclusively for the institutional ethos (*"Rehabilitamos con amor"*), creating an intimate human contract without compromising technical medical authority.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative clinical stability with dynamic accessibility:

- **Primary Deep Medical Blue (`#0A3D91`):** Represents clinical precision, neurological and orthopedic authority, and structural trust. Used for dominant brand anchors, navigation headers, structural outlines, key badges, and primary clinical actions.
- **Secondary Golden Amber (`#FFC107`):** High-energy, warm accent chosen for critical urgency, triage focal points, and prominent conversion triggers. Transition states shift into `#E0A800`.
- **Tertiary Direct Care Green (`#25D366`):** Dedicated exclusively to immediate patient support pathways and real-time WhatsApp triage. Pair with `#128C7E` on active interactions.
- **Neutral Deep Slate (`#12263F`):** Provides AA/AAA accessibility compliance for all display headings and dense body copy, avoiding harsh absolute black.
- **Secondary Neutral Cool Slate (`#4A5568`):** Applied to supporting clinical metadata, service tags, captions, and secondary details.
- **Atmospheric Canvas (`#FFFFFF`, `#F7FAFD`, `#EEF4FB`):** Backgrounds transition softly from clean clinical white into subtle ice-blue linear washes (180deg vertical gradients), framing content cards without harsh separator borders.

## Typography

The type scale centers on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, providing clarity through friendly geometric terminals, tall x-height, and open apertures that support legibility across varying reading conditions and age groups.

- **Headlines & Display:** Strong negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`700` and `800`) provide structure and visual priority.
- **Body & Clinical Records:** Uncluttered vertical rhythm (`1.5` to `1.6` line-height) set in neutral high-contrast ink (`#12263F`) ensures sustained readability during consultation reviews.
- **Institutional Motto ("Rehabilitamos con amor"):** Rendered using **Playfair Display** in italicized, medium-weighted styling. This adds an editorial, handcrafted human finish that contrasts with the geometric structure of the clinical services, without loading extraneous external script files that degrade network performance.

## Layout & Spacing

A responsive 12-column grid system drives desktop and tablet experiences, folding into a 4-column framework for mobile:

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, max container width of 1280px, centered margins (`3rem`), gutters at `1.5rem`. Service matrices and clinical specialty portfolios distribute comfortably across 3 or 4-column spans.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `2rem` outer margins, `1.25rem` gutters. Interactive cards reflow into 2-column modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1.25rem` screen margins, `1rem` gutters. Touch components expand horizontally, while appointment booking steps transition into vertical progress tracks.

Vertical rhythm adheres strictly to an 8px base unit. Generous section separation (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) allows cognitive resting spaces between complex therapy descriptions, specialized clinical procedures, and admission paperwork.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids harsh dropshadows or heavy outlines, leaning instead on airy, therapeutic light refraction:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Base `#FFFFFF` or subtle gradient fields (`linear-gradient(180deg, #FFFFFF 0%, #F7FAFD 60%, #EEF4FB 100%)`).
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Clinical Tiles):** `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(10, 61, 145, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(18, 38, 63, 0.03)`. A blue-tinted ambient shadow creates lift without muddy grays. Outlines use a soft edge: `1px solid rgba(10, 61, 145, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover State & Active Modals):** `0 16px 32px -4px rgba(10, 61, 145, 0.10), 0 6px 12px -2px rgba(18, 38, 63, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a subtle `-4px` vertical translate on interactive cards.
- **Level 3 (Fixed Navigation & Urgent Floating Triggers):** WhatsApp and quick-triage floaters sit above the content canvas with an amber/green aura: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.35)`.
- **Glassmorphism Backdrop:** Navigation headers use a frosted panel surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) to keep appointments and emergency buttons visible while scrolling.

## Shapes

The interface embraces generous, welcoming curves that soften clinical friction and communicate approachable care:

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Form controls, date pickers, utility inputs, and micro-badges.
- **Card Radius (1rem - 1.5rem / 16px - 24px):** Service containers, therapy overviews, specialist profiles, and facility highlights (`rounded-2xl`).
- **Feature Surfaces (1.5rem - 2rem / 24px - 32px):** Hero callouts, emergency contact panels, and patient portal wrappers (`rounded-3xl`).
- **Pill Radius (9999px):** Primary conversion buttons, WhatsApp triggers, tag filters, and phone hotlines.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Clinical Action:** Solid `#0A3D91` fill, crisp `#FFFFFF` text, `border-radius: 9999px`, horizontal padding of `1.75rem`, vertical padding of `0.875rem`. Hover state introduces `#082F72` with an ambient blue glow.
- **Urgent / Direct WhatsApp Action:** High-saturation `#FFC107` (or `#25D366` for dedicated chat channels), `#12263F` typography for readability on amber, or `#FFFFFF` on green. Interactive elevation triggers a micro-scale of `1.02` with an active focus ring.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#0A3D91`, `#0A3D91` text. On hover, shifts to `rgba(10, 61, 145, 0.04)`.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Wide aspect ratios with `1.5rem` internal padding on mobile, expanding to `2.25rem` on desktop.
- Structural white core (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a gentle `1px solid rgba(10, 61, 145, 0.08)` border.
- Integrated specialty icon badges seated inside soft circular ice-blue wells (`#EEF4FB`).
- Hover shifts card upwards by `-4px`, transitioning the subtle border into a richer tone of primary blue (`rgba(10, 61, 145, 0.20)`).

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Height set to `3rem` to support easy touch targeting for patients with motor limitations.
- Border: `1.5px solid #E2E8F0` on `#FFFFFF` fill; focus transitions instantly to `2px solid #0A3D91` with a `0 0 0 4px rgba(10, 61, 145, 0.12)` halo.
- Labels stay top-aligned in `label-md` using `#12263F` to maintain permanent context during completion.

### Chips & Specialties Filters
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `space-sm` vertical by `space-md` horizontal padding.
- Unselected: `#EEF4FB` background with `#0A3D91` text.
- Active: `#0A3D91` solid background with pure white typography.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: `1.25rem x 1.25rem` with `rounded-md` (checkboxes) and `rounded-full` (radios).
- Border: `2px solid #0A3D91`. Checked state transitions to a solid `#0A3D91` fill housing a pure white check icon.

### Floating Urgent WhatsApp Drawer
- Pinned at bottom-right viewport coordinates (`bottom: 2rem`, `right: 2rem`).
- Circular touch target of `3.75rem` featuring an embedded direct message bubble indicator.
- Accompanying tooltip: `"¿Necesitas terapia o consulta? Escríbenos"` rendered with Level 2 elevation for immediate user orientation.
